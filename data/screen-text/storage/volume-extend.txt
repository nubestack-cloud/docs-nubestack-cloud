It is 20 GiB now. A volume can only grow, never shrink, and it must be detached. The larger size is charged from the moment it is applied. The disk inside your instance does not grow by itself: once it is attached again, grow its partition and file system there (for example growpart and resize2fs, or Disk Management on Windows). Find the disk by its label or UUID, not a name like /dev/vdb, which can change between attachments.
